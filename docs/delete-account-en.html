Penguin Hockey: delete your account

한국어

Developer: YS Games · Contact: choiys6421@gmail.com

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Penguin Hockey and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Manage on the Account · cloud save row.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your cloud save and Google sign-in information are deleted right away and you are signed out on the device.

If you cannot use the app (request by email)

  1. Send an email to choiys6421@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Account deletion request and write the Google account email you signed in with.
  3. We delete the account and cloud save within 7 days of confirming the request and reply by email.

To confirm it is you, we may check that the request comes from the same address as the Google account.

What is deleted

  • Google account information and anonymous user ID
  • Cloud save (points, records, players, settings, etc.)
  • Friend match room information and attendance record (if any remain in the cloud)

What stays on the device

The game save stored on your device can remain after you delete your account in the app. To remove it completely, uninstall the app or go to Settings → Apps → Penguin Hockey → Storage → Clear data.

Purchases

In-app purchases such as the Pro Pass are managed by Google Play. To see or cancel a purchase, use Payments & subscriptions in the Google Play Store. The developer never receives or stores card details.
