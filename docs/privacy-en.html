Penguin Hockey Privacy Policy

한국어 · Effective: November 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 14, 2026

YS Games ("the developer") provides Penguin Hockey ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version applies.

1. Information collected

ItemWhenPurpose
Google account information (email, display name)If you choose to sign in with GoogleIdentify your account, link your cloud save
Anonymous user IDCreated automatically the first time you use friend matchIdentify players in a match room, link your cloud save
Game save data (points, records, players, settings, etc.)Saved automatically while you playSave on the device; back up to the cloud if you are signed in
Friend match information (room code, connection details during the match)When you play a friend matchConnect the two devices and run the match
Attendance record (last check-in date, total check-ins)When you claim attendance rewardsGive attendance rewards

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. It uses no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics.

2. How it is used

  • Sign-in, account checks, cloud save backup and restore
  • Connecting and running friend matches
  • Game features such as attendance rewards and growth stories
  • Purchases (Pro Pass) are processed by Google Play. The developer does not use purchase information for sales or advertising.

3. How long it is kept

  • Save on the device: stays on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Android backup: the device save may be included in your Google account backup through Android's built-in backup, so your progress can move to a new phone. The developer cannot see this backup. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.
  • Cloud save and account information: kept until you delete your account.
  • Friend match room information: used only for the match and not used afterwards. Deleted together with your account on request.
  • Information that must be kept by law is kept for the period the law requires.

4. Service providers

The developer does not sell your information or share it for advertising. The app uses these services to run:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, realtime database): account checks, cloud save, friend match connection
  • Google Play (billing, sign-in screen): payment processing and Google sign-in
  • STUN server (provided by Google): finds addresses so two devices can connect directly. If that fails, the match is relayed through Firebase.

5. Security

Traffic between the device and the servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Firebase security rules allow a cloud save to be read and written only by its own account.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can view, correct and delete your information and delete your account. See the account deletion page for details.

  • In the app: Settings → Account · cloud save → Manage → Delete account
  • If you cannot use the app: email the address below and we will handle your request.

7. Players under 14

Anyone can play the game, whatever their age. Players under 14 should play without signing in (progress is saved on the device). Google sign-in, cloud save and friend match process personal information and need a parent or guardian’s consent; if we learn that such information was collected without consent, we delete it without delay.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it in the app or on this page and update the effective date.
